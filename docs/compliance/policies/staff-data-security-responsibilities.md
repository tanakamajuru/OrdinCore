# Staff data security responsibilities — OrdinCore

> DSPT Standard 2. Every person with access to OrdinCore systems or care data must read this and sign
> the acknowledgement below. Owner: **[NEEDS INPUT]**. Review: annually.

## Your responsibilities

1. **Access only what you need** for your job. Never view or share care data out of curiosity or for
   any reason other than legitimate work.
2. **Keep credentials private.** Never share your password or MFA device. Never log in as someone
   else or let someone use your session.
3. **Use strong, unique passwords** and enrol in MFA when prompted (mandatory for managers/admins).
4. **Lock your screen** when away from your device. Do not leave care data visible to others.
5. **Report incidents immediately** — a suspected breach, lost/stolen device, phishing email, or
   accidental disclosure — to **[NEEDS INPUT: incident contact]** (see
   [../breach-and-incident-response.md](../breach-and-incident-response.md)). Reporting fast is never
   penalised; hiding a problem is.
6. **No secrets in code or chat.** Credentials live only in server environment variables
   ([../../operations/secret-management.md](../../operations/secret-management.md)).
7. **Devices**: use up-to-date, supported devices with screen lock and disk encryption for any access.
8. **Leavers/movers**: access is removed on the last working day; tell **[NEEDS INPUT]** of any role
   change so access can be adjusted (least privilege).
9. **Data stays in approved systems** — do not copy care data to personal email, personal cloud
   storage, USB sticks or spreadsheets outside OrdinCore.

## System administrators (elevated access) — additional (DSPT v8)

If you hold admin/super-admin or infrastructure access you additionally acknowledge that your access
is **privileged and high-risk**: you will use it only for authorised administration, keep MFA on at
all times, never use shared admin accounts, and accept that your administrative actions are logged and
reviewed.
