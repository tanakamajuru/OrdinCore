OrdinCore Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 August 2026

This policy explains how personal data is handled in the OrdinCore mobile application and service (“OrdinCore”, “the app”, “we”). OrdinCore is a governance and compliance tool for adult social care providers.

1. Who is responsible for your data

OrdinCore is operated by Gella Care Services (proprietor: Mr Kudakwashe Sikangela), 20 Caldecote Road, Eynesbury, St. Neots, PE19 2UH, United Kingdom.

  • For account data about the professionals who use the app (name, work email, role), Gella Care Services is the data controller.
  • For care governance records that a care organisation records in OrdinCore about the people it supports and its staff (signals, risks, actions, notes, incidents), the care organisation is the data controller and OrdinCore acts as its data processor under a written agreement.

Contact for privacy matters: admin@gellacareservices.net, tel. +44 7564 303108.

3. How we use data and our lawful bases (UK GDPR)

  • Providing the service — performance of a contract and our / the care organisation’s legitimate interests in safe, well‑governed care.
  • Generating governance narratives and reports — text you enter may be processed by a third‑party AI provider acting as our sub‑processor solely to draft report wording; it is not used to train third‑party models.
  • Notifications — to alert you to tasks, escalations and briefs.
  • Security, troubleshooting and legal compliance.

Care records may include special‑category data (e.g. health or safeguarding information). Where OrdinCore processes this on behalf of a care organisation, that organisation is responsible for the lawful basis (typically the provision of health or social care, and safeguarding of individuals at risk).

4. Who we share data with

We share personal data only with service providers who help us run OrdinCore, under contract and only as needed:

  • Cloud hosting — to store and serve the application and its database.
  • AI narrative provider — to draft report and review wording from the data you supply.
  • Push‑notification delivery.

We may disclose data if required by law or to protect someone’s vital interests (e.g. safeguarding). We do not sell data or share it for advertising.

5. Security

All data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. Access is restricted by role‑based permissions and authenticated accounts. We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the information we hold.

6. Retention

Governance records are retained for as long as the care organisation’s account is active and as required by its record‑keeping and regulatory obligations, then deleted or anonymised. Account data is kept while your account is active.

7. Your rights

Subject to UK GDPR you may request access to, correction of, deletion of, or restriction of your personal data, and object to certain processing. To exercise these rights, or to request deletion of your account and associated personal data, email admin@gellacareservices.net. For care records held on behalf of a care organisation, please also contact that organisation as the controller. You may complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).

8. Account & data deletion

You can request deletion of your OrdinCore account and associated personal data at any time by emailing admin@gellacareservices.net. We will action verified requests in line with applicable law and our processing agreements.

9. Children

OrdinCore is a workplace tool for care professionals and is not directed at, or intended for use by, children.

10. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the “last updated” date above. Material changes will be communicated through the service.
