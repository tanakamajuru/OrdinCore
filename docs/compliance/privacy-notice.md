**Who we are.** **[PROVIDER LEGAL NAME]** ("we") uses OrdinCore, a care‑governance platform, to
record and oversee the quality and safety of the care we provide. We are the **data controller**.
Contact our Data Protection Officer at **[DPO EMAIL / ADDRESS]**. ICO registration **[NUMBER]**.

**What we collect.**
- *About people we support:* name/initials, the service you live in, and information relevant to
  your care and safety — including health, mental‑health, behaviour, medication and safeguarding
  information, and, where recorded as evidence, photographs or short voice notes.
- *About staff:* name, work email, role, the service(s) you are assigned to, and login information.

**Why we use it & our lawful basis.** To deliver, monitor and improve safe care and to meet our
regulatory (CQC) duties. Our lawful bases are **[Art. 6(1)(e) public task / (f) legitimate
interests — CONFIRM]**, and for health information **Art. 9(2)(h) (health and social care)**.

**Who we share it with.** Our staff on a need‑to‑know basis; our processors who run the platform
and supporting services (hosting, email, and — where enabled — text‑generation), listed in our
[subprocessor list](./subprocessors.md); and regulators/authorities where legally required. We do
**not** sell your data.

**International transfers.** Data is hosted in the **UK**. Some supporting services may process data
outside the UK; where they do, we use appropriate safeguards (UK IDTA/Addendum). See the
subprocessor list. **[CONFIRM — especially any text‑generation/AI feature.]**

**How long we keep it.** We keep records only as long as necessary for care and legal/regulatory
purposes — see our [retention schedule](./data-retention-and-deletion.md). **[CONFIRM PERIODS]**

**How we protect it.** Access is restricted by role and service; evidence files are only accessible
through authenticated, access‑logged requests; data is encrypted in transit; backups are encrypted;
sessions can be revoked. See our security measures.

**Your rights.** You have the right to be informed, and (subject to conditions) to access, rectify,
erase, restrict, object to, and port your data. To exercise them, contact **[DPO EMAIL]**. You can
complain to the ICO (ico.org.uk).
