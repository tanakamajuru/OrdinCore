**Parties:** Controller **[PROVIDER LEGAL NAME]**; Processor **[ORDINCORE OPERATING ENTITY]**.

## 1. Subject matter & duration
The Processor processes personal data solely to provide the OrdinCore platform for the term of the
service agreement and this DPA.

## 2. Nature & purpose; data & data subjects
As set out in the [DPIA](./DPIA.md): governance recording and oversight of care; special‑category
(health/mental‑health) data about service users and account data about staff.

## 3. Controller instructions
The Processor processes personal data only on the Controller's documented instructions (this DPA and
the platform's configured functionality), including for international transfers, unless required by
law (in which case it notifies the Controller unless legally prohibited).

## 4. Confidentiality
Personnel authorised to process the data are bound by confidentiality.

## 5. Security (Art. 32)
The Processor maintains appropriate technical and organisational measures, including: tenant
isolation by organisation; role‑ and site‑based access control; authenticated, access‑logged
evidence delivery; encryption in transit; encrypted backups; refresh‑token rotation/revocation and
fail‑closed sessions; login throttling; security‑event logging. See the security measures summary.

## 6. Subprocessors
The Controller grants **general authorisation** for the subprocessors listed in
[subprocessors.md](./subprocessors.md). The Processor imposes equivalent data‑protection terms on
each and remains liable for them, and will give the Controller **[e.g. 14 days]** notice of any
intended addition/replacement, allowing objection.

## 7. Data subject rights & assistance
The Processor assists the Controller (taking account of the nature of processing) to respond to data
subject requests and to meet Art. 32–36 obligations, including breach notification and DPIAs.

## 8. Personal data breach
The Processor notifies the Controller **without undue delay and within [24–48] hours** of becoming
aware of a personal data breach, with the information in the
[breach & incident procedure](./breach-and-incident-response.md).

## 9. International transfers
No transfer outside the UK except as listed in the subprocessor list under an appropriate safeguard
(UK IDTA/Addendum + transfer risk assessment). **AI/LLM (OpenAI) transfers require explicit Controller
authorisation and safeguards — see DPIA R3.**

## 10. Return & deletion
On termination, at the Controller's choice, the Processor returns or securely deletes personal data
(including from backups on their expiry cycle) and certifies deletion, unless retention is legally
required. See [retention & deletion](./data-retention-and-deletion.md).

## 11. Audits
The Processor makes available information necessary to demonstrate compliance and allows/contributes
to audits **[frequency / notice]**.
